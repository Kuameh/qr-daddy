QR Daddy design system v1.0

A calm tool with a loud voice. The generator is neutral and precise; personality lives in the heavy display type, hard ink shadows on stickers and the primary action, and the codes themselves. Cobalt is the only accent. Mirrors DESIGN_SYSTEM.md.
